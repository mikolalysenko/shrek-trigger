## Current scope and roadmap

The November 1 design revision narrows the remaining art goal. **Generic/random enemies and non-story monster variants keep their original Chrono Trigger art.** Do not commission, integrate or acceptance-test further mob reskins. Custom visual work remains for the playable cast, named or plot-important characters and bosses, Dragon, story-critical props/cinematics and environment work needed by the adapted story. Existing out-of-scope mob replacements recorded below are historical implementation work, not requirements to preserve or finish. On November 6 the user tightened this further: custom sprites stay only for the playable cast, characters critical to the plot and direct Shrek references. The Kilwala, Hench, household residents, townswomen, bats, jugglers, bridge skeletons, sewer frogs, Proto robots, Mage, court officer and Reptites, plus the route bosses Krawlie, Heckran, Zombor, Nizbel and the Guardian, are stock again; see the [decision log](decisions.md). Milestone entries below that describe those redraws are history.

The former kingdom of Zeal is now **Ohio**. The remaining campaign work must treat that as a coherent rebrand of player-facing captions, dialogue, setting themes and story-relevant NPCs, while preserving the native maps, encounters, choices and progression. Native `zeal` identifiers and historical evidence may retain the old technical name.

The technique revision preserves **77 stock techniques and scopes 39 custom or explicitly modified techniques**. Stock magic retains its exact Chrono Trigger name, animation and mechanics except for the explicitly approved **Swamp Belch** cloud and **Hot Sauce** blast. Puss and Mongo exchange Charm/Heal, Puss receives Cat Kiss and Nine Lives, and four combinations change participants to match. Swamp Belch is integrated and shares its reserved native effect hooks with Yarn Bomb; Hot Sauce retains Flare's damage, 20 MP cost, targets and progression through the native Mega Bomb scene. Ogre Roar, Storm Breath and Get Up remain historical experiments. All 39 scoped techniques now have native presentations; independent-emulator, interruption and connected-progression acceptance remains.

The checklist at the end of this file is the current completion bar. The milestone entries between this section and that checklist are a historical evidence log: their dates, cartridge hashes, test results and then-current remaining counts describe those builds, not today's scope. The latest commits after the Four Layer Slam milestone also replace Dragon's native presentations, fix the browser title's signed Mode 7 offsets, and give Shrek a generated onion kart; their focused source checks do not change the full-campaign acceptance gaps below.
