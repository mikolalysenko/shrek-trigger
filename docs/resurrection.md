# Steve's 24-egg challenge

Steve Harwell replaces Gaspar at the Last Inn and in the displaced-guru scene. His sprite source is a built-in imagegen atlas at `assets/imagegen/steve/source-v1.png`; its exact prompt, hash, extraction bounds and 12-color conversion are stored beside it. NPC 72 retains its eight original frame roles and adds an egg-at-mouth frame 8 and chewing frame 9. NPC 188 retains two frames. The existing four 16×16 sprite cells and animation tables remain in use. In the Last Inn, both Steve actors share OBJ palette 7 with identical colors. This replaces Gaspar's original use of Robo's palette 3 and leaves all seven party palettes intact.

After Shrek's loss, the original memorial and departure reminder still lead to the Time Egg conversation. The player can antagonize Steve into eating 24 ordinary eggs or leave him alone. Declining restores control and awards nothing. Accepting plays 24 blocking native bite/chew pairs, with exchanges after 6, 12, 18 and 24 eggs. Only then does the original handoff grant item 217, Second Chance. Its existing saved flags prevent another reward; the Three Pigs' guidance, stand-in requirement and Death Peak rescue remain the downstream native quest.

`engine/resurrection.js` replaces two event-function pointers inside location 464. It shares identical, return-terminated helpers to fit the original 5888-byte decompressed event buffer. A helper cannot be folded if execution could fall through to it or a relative branch enters, leaves or crosses it. Every unchanged function is checked against its original byte sequence, apart from its relocated text table and Steve's explicit palette-slot change. The event decoder validates instruction boundaries before and after compaction. No extra WRAM, VRAM, CPU hook or new saved flag is needed.

`content/resurrection.js` owns seven new local text IDs 240–246. Original Last Inn choices retain their option rows. `content/inn-dialogue.js` supplies Steve's other conversations; the late-game rewrite distinguishes the 24 ordinary eggs from the Time Egg that he gives the party.

In the browser workshop, **Resurrection · Steve remembers Shrek** starts before the memorial, and **Resurrection · Steve and the 24 eggs** prepares the original handoff prerequisite. These are reversible diagnostics; Return restores the previous party, story and inventory. Once the transition has occurred, normal controller input and cartridge code run the scene.

`node --test tests/resurrection.test.mjs` checks the native buffer, text ownership, refusal, all 24 displayed bite/chew pairs, reward, repeat protection and undo. `npm run test:resurrection-cores` runs the same controller-only acceptance route in Snes9x and bsnes, recording screenshots and exact ROM hashes. The complete memorial-to-Death-Peak route, alternate parties and save/reload continuation remain part of the campaign acceptance work.
