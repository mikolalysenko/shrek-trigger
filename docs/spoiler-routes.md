# Route and ending notes — spoilers

This file is intentionally separate from the quick-start guide. Read it only after finishing the game or if you want route spoilers.

## Final guardian

The small living starter is the true target. Defeating only Tyson cannot win. Its rebuild restores protection, so use the ward-broken damage window and destroy the starter even if Tyson remains standing.

Tyson's tell uses text and a stable charge pose as well as sound. The selected living target receives one defense-independent knockout after the windup. Defeating or disabling Tyson during that windup cancels the queued strike where supported; a changed target also cancels it. Use the recovery window to revive and heal. Onion Juice is the reliable revival supply.

Shrek's swamp line requires a living Shrek in the active party. Universal starter hints do not require Donkey.

## Dragon and ending variations

Dragon's temporal harness initially permits time jumps; the builders' wing repair enables ordinary flight. Board through the native vehicle prompt. The final dive breaks the harness but shows Dragon escaping alive. An intact Dragon changes the ending travel presentation rather than her survival.

The ending matrix retains:

- Shrek present or absent, including the Second Chance rescue route.
- Farquaad recruited or defeated; recruitment does not require forgiveness.
- Dragon intact or used for the final dive.
- Early-finale and New Game Plus access through the native bucket/gate routes.
- Party-dependent farewells and silent presentations whose casting, rather than added prose, carries the scene.

The Successor wedding-album scene, Reunion rescue, Dream Project, The Oath, Prophet, Slide Show, Apocalypse, and Dino Age variations have adapted text. Slide Show's final joke assumes Shrek has been speaking throughout the adventure.

## Optional quests

Retain the Carnival Hatchet repair and awakening, Pinocchio/Magic Mirror identity conflict, swamp reclamation, Fiona/Harold reconciliation, Puss/Sir Bram, Mongo's community, and Horner/enforcer encounters. Special Buttons select their gated triple techniques only when their required party and single-tech prerequisites are present.

Steve's resurrection handoff requires accepting and finishing all 24 ordinary eggs. Refusal leaves the challenge available. The stand-in and mountain rescue remain necessary after Second Chance; the egg challenge alone does not resurrect Shrek.

## Optional superboss: Mr. Worldwide

After the Wish Palace (the original Ocean Palace), a lost Walmart greeter waits on the right side of Guy Fieri's lesson room at the End of Time. Talk to him and accept the shortcut to Kodiak, Alaska. Pitbull has lived in that Walmart's stockroom since the internet voted to send him there, and he calls it his exile. Saying no to either of them is always safe. Every way out of the stockroom (leaving, winning or losing) brings you back to Guy's kitchen, and losing has no game over. Pitbull patches the party up, and the greeter will take you back for a rematch.

He has 30,000 HP and four phases at HP thresholds, each with its own weakness. He tells you all four before the fight:

- Miami heat: Water. He absorbs Fire.
- Penthouse suite: Air and physical hits; his magic defense is high, Fire is halved and he can heal himself (Hotel Room Service).
- Fireball mode: Fire, though every Fire hit hypes him up and raises his attack. He absorbs Water.
- Kodiak exile: Dirt; he absorbs Fire and halves the rest.

Nearly all his attacks are Fire, so Fire protection pays off. "He is counting down! Brace!" and one idle turn always come before FIREBALL, his strongest party-wide attack, so heal or defend then. Victory pays:
- 9,999 XP, 15,000 onions and 100 TP;
- a Swamp Feast drop;
- his shades (Spectacular Specs, +50% damage) and three more Swamp Feasts.

Puss can Charm a Pinhead from him. The reward plays once; New Game Plus brings him back.

## Save routes for alternate endings

Use a real save point before a mutually exclusive choice. Do not rely on emulator savestates for ordinary play. Load the native slot after the choice to explore another route; battery slots are independent. As with the original game, changing development patches can make an older save unsuitable, so keep the matching BPS fingerprint.
