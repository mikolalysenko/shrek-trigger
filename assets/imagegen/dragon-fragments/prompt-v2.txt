BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT, most important: the entire background of the image, everywhere outside the sprites, is one single flat solid pure cyan color (#00FFFF), perfectly uniform, like a chroma-key screen. No gradient, no texture, no glow, no shadow.

Tiny pixel-art effect sprites for a 16-bit SNES JRPG: a torn fragment of a dragon's leather flight harness, tumbling through the air. Each sprite is drawn at a true resolution of only 14 pixels wide by 24 pixels tall, then enlarged so that every single pixel is a large, perfectly square, hard-edged block of one flat color (no anti-aliasing, no texture or scratches inside the leather, no sub-pixel detail).

The design: a ragged flag-like strip of deep crimson leather with a one-block dark maroon outline, two flat crimson shades and a rose-pink highlight edge along the top; a square tan-gold buckle plate 5 blocks wide with a single pale pink glint block in its center; a thin loose strap end 1 to 2 blocks wide dangling below, ending in a tan tip. Use at most 8 colors: dark maroon, two crimsons, rose, pink, pale pink, tan and dark brown.

Arrange two rows of three: each row shows the same fragment tumbling, each frame tilted a little further and with the strap end swinging further to the right. The two rows are alternative versions with slightly different tears. Every sprite is the same size, evenly spaced and fully separated.
