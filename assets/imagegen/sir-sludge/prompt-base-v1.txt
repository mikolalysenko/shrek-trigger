Entire crown, ears, hands, mace and boots visible. Compact upright silhouette suitable for a body about 44 pixels tall and 28 to 31 pixels wide at final native size. Moss-green skin in four shades, charcoal blue-gray iron in three shades, warm dark brown leather in two shades, tiny muted brass and ivory highlights.
