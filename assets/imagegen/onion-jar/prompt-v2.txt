A single 16-bit SNES-era RPG pixel art prop in the style of Chrono Trigger field sprites: a squat, round earthenware clay jar with a wide mouth, filled to the brim with a heap of big, pale yellowish-cream curved toenail clippings, crescent shaped, piled like coins in a savings jar, a few spilling over the rim. Cartoonish and gross-funny, not realistic. Seen from a three-quarter top-down angle, sitting upright, centered, filling most of the frame. Chunky readable shapes, limited palette of warm browns, tans, cream and pale yellow, a dark brown outline one pixel wide, simple two-tone shading. No text, no ground shadow, no other objects. Perfectly flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) background.
