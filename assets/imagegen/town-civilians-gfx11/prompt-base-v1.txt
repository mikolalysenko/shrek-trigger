Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production sprite source atlas for an actual SNES JRPG cartridge.
Create a new original Duloc fairy-tale town keeper, a versatile ordinary adult man who works as a shopkeeper, innkeeper or civic clerk. This is a new character design, not a recolor or tracing of an existing game sprite. He has a warm expressive face, broad small nose, dark brows, neat brown side hair, a restrained short brown moustache, and a soft rounded blue-teal felt cap. Wear a short ochre-rust waistcoat over an ivory open-collar linen shirt, olive-green trousers, a dark belt and practical dark shoes. Empty hands, no apron, no armor, no carried trade tools, no celebrity likeness.
Style: carefully clustered Chrono Trigger SNES pixel art, compact readable anatomy, slightly large head, nuanced three-tone cloth/skin shading, strong silhouette, consistent top-left light, no smooth gradients, no vector outlines. Design for a 32-pixel tall full body, approximately 17-pixel front width and 13-pixel profile width. The raster may be enlarged, but the silhouette and face must read at that logical scale. The cap and clothing are symmetric so front/rear phases may be mirrored.
Composition: exactly NINE separate complete figures in a regular 3-column by 3-row atlas on genuinely transparent background. All nine use identical model, scale, palette and camera. Generous transparent corridors, no clipped shoes or cap, no contact shadows, no labels, no text, no lines, no scenery.
Camera: top-down three-quarter SNES field-game camera. Front/back show the top of the cap slightly; profiles face screen LEFT.
Row 1: (1) FRONT idle, both feet grounded; (2) BACK idle, face entirely hidden; (3) LEFT profile idle, neutral feet.
Row 2: (4) BACK walking contact A, left boot forward and right boot back with opposed arms; (5) BACK walking passing phase after that contact, one heel lifted and legs passing beneath body, distinct from both idle and contact; (6) FRONT walking contact A, left boot forward and right boot back with opposed arms.
Row 3: (7) FRONT walking passing phase after that contact, one heel lifted and legs passing beneath body, distinct from both idle and contact; (8) LEFT profile walking contact A, near leg forward and far leg back, near arm back; (9) LEFT profile walking contact B, near leg back and far leg forward, near arm forward. The last two must have visibly opposite arm and leg contacts, not duplicated poses.
Palette direction: charcoal, ivory, light peach, warm tan, brown, ochre orange, rust, muted light teal, slate blue, olive green, dark olive, cool grey. Keep materials distinct using a compact palette. Transparency is real alpha.
