Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production sprite source for a Chrono Trigger-style SNES role-playing game.
Primary request: exactly three distinct animation poses of one small Sugar Ward, a floating protective crystal formed from caramelized sugar. It should look like a carefully shaded SNES enemy sprite, with crisp clustered pixels and readable forms at a logical 32 by 32 pixels per pose.
Subject: one compact upright translucent rock-sugar crystal with a broad irregular faceted upper body, tapered lower point, a bright warm cream heart, amber and caramel planes, tiny burgundy internal fissures. It is a serious magical ward within a strange fairy-tale bakery, not jewelry, a UI icon, a character with eyes, or a flat symmetrical diamond. Keep a clear outer silhouette and nuanced small pixel facets.
Composition: one horizontal row, exactly three equal square cells, no text or dividers. Each cell contains one complete crystal, centered, with generous transparent margins and no contact with another cell. Same identity, size, facing and upper-left lighting in all three cells. Body should occupy about 24 by 27 logical pixels inside a 32 by 32 cell.
Left pose: resting upright crystal, calm warm central glow.
Middle pose: the same crystal lifted very slightly as the central glow grows, with the large right facet turning brighter and a narrow darker left facet. Keep the point and crown complete.
Right pose: brightest pulse, a subtly changed facet angle and two tiny close sugar glints, with a visibly distinct bright internal vein. This is still the intact crystal, not destruction or a different object. The animation will play left, middle, right, middle at ten ticks each.
Style: authentic late-SNES JRPG pixel art in the aesthetic of Chrono Trigger; tightly grouped highlights and shadows, restrained dark outline, no soft gradients, no antialiasing, no photographic reflections, no ambient cast shadow, no floor. Do not make a modern high-resolution painted illustration disguised by a grid. Render as a small native pixel sprite enlarged at an integer scale.
Color palette: transparent plus only these twelve opaque RGB colors: #211b22 #593426 #975535 #ce8c55 #751b30 #cf3541 #f3dfba #252735 #4b5272 #e3b65c #f5c783 #fff5d2. Warm ivory and amber must dominate the crystal. All background must be genuinely transparent, including between poses; no checkerboard or colored background.
Constraints: exactly three complete poses, consistent scale and light, no lettering, labels, UI, logos, watermark, border, extra objects, clipped points or detached wide effects.
