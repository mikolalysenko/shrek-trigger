Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production sprite source for a Chrono Trigger-style SNES role-playing game.
Primary request: exactly three distinct animation poses of one small living Cosmic Muffin starter. This is the tiny living core of an immense fairy-tale bakery monster, not its outer shell. Make a carefully shaded late-SNES enemy sprite with crisp clustered pixels and readable forms at a logical 32 by 32 pixels per pose.
Subject: a squat small muffin with a broad irregular domed crust, a ribbed deep-brown paper-like baked wrapper, several dark chocolate-like craters, caramel baked edges, and small warm ivory starter fissures glowing inside the crust. No limbs, face, hat, plate, flame plume or separate character.
Composition: one horizontal row of exactly three equal square cells, no text or dividers. Each cell contains one complete muffin, centered with generous transparent margins and no overlap. Same front three-quarter view, identity, proportions and upper-left light in all three poses. Its base remains centered. Whole muffin occupies about 26 by 25 logical pixels inside a 32 by 32 cell.
Left pose: relaxed rounded muffin, compact crust and faint warm fissures.
Middle pose: gentle inhaling phase, crust rises slightly, wrapper ribbing stretches, the warm central fissure widens a little. Retain the same craters and folds in the same places.
Right pose: peak breath, slightly fuller domed crust and a bright but bounded internal starter glow between crust cracks. Distinguish it clearly from the first two at native size without opening a huge mouth or changing object identity. The animation plays left, middle, right, middle at ten ticks each, so each phase must connect naturally in both directions.
Style: authentic Chrono Trigger-era SNES pixel art, clustered directional shading, restrained dark outlines and clean readable baked texture. No soft gradients, antialiasing, photographic detail, cast shadow, floor or background. Render as a small native pixel sprite enlarged by an integer scale, not a smooth illustration disguised by a grid.

Constraints: exactly three complete poses, consistent small body scale, no labels, lettering, UI, logo, watermark, border, extra objects, clipped crust or wide effects.
