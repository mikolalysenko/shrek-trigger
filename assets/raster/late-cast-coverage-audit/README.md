The PNGs show **original Chrono Trigger source art** to establish the work still required.

The next approved pair is **Rumpelstiltskin for Dalton/Dalton Plus**, and **enchanted wishworks constructs for Golem/Golem Boss**. The pose audit is in `pose-notes.json`. Rumpelstiltskin needs four genuine directional gaits, the three airborne cloak states, a crouch, reactions and all turning/gesture sequences. There are 40 nonempty facing/mode lists across 52 entries. The golems have 28 nonempty lists across 36 entries, all four directions intentionally sharing the same frontal action views. Their hover, jaw contraction, arm release and reaction particles must remain distinct. New art must preserve native timing and signed cell coordinates; no statistics, AI, rewards, progression, movement or collision changes are proposed.

Replacement generation will use the recognizable Rumpelstiltskin model, then a coherent fairy-tale wishworks construct suited to the existing gold and muted rose palette variants. This differs from the already completed Mire/Iron Golem family at graphics 154 / assembly 147. The golems' narrow crown cell and separated hands require source-to-cell coverage checks. Their decoded bank must stay at or below the already proven 32 KiB streaming bound. Growth, heap/co-load proof and original twelve-cell OAM priority remain integration checks, not assumptions from this audit.
